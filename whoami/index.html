> Roadmap

> SOC Operations & Incident Response

Detecting, triaging and containing threats across multi-vendor environments.

I turn noisy alerts into clear decisions: what happened, how bad it is, and what to do next. Always with an eye on DFIR.

What I do:

  • Monitor and triage security alerts, escalating confirmed incidents through playbooks and escalation procedures.
  • Execute and recommend initial mitigation and containment actions alongside client teams and L2/L3 analysts.
  • Tune detection rules and improve use cases to cut false positives and raise alert quality and coverage.
  • Adapt quickly to each environment across different SIEM, EDR and XDR platforms.
  • Document incidents with clear, concise tickets and handovers that clients and teams can act on.

> Infrastructure Security

Building networks that are secure by default.

Defense starts at the architecture: segment it, harden it, automate it, and keep it available.

What I do:

  • Design and deploy network architectures, from SOHO to organizational, with VLAN segmentation, ACLs, QoS and load-balanced firewalls.
  • Apply advanced hardening and access controls to reduce the attack surface and exposure to automated threats.
  • Automate security processes with scripts and internal tools, speeding up operations and response.
